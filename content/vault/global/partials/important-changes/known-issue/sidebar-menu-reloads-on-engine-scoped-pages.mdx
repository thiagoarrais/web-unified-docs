### Sidebar menu reloads on engine-scoped pages ((#sidebar-menu-reloads-on-engine-scoped-pages))

| Change      | Status | Vault edition | Affected version | Fixed version |
| ----------- | ------ | ------------- | ---------------- | ------------- |
| Known issue | Open   | All           | 2.0.0            | No            |

#### Issue

On some engine-scoped UI routes, the Vault sidebar can briefly render the wrong
menu or reload during navigation before settling on the correct section.

This is most noticeable on pages such as **Operational tools -> Custom
messages** and secret-detail routes. The issue is limited to transient sidebar
flicker during page transitions and does not affect the underlying operation.

#### Recommendation

There is no direct workaround for this sidebar behavior.

Users can continue with the affected workflow after the page finishes loading
and the sidebar settles into the correct state.
